Hi there! I'm a Research Assistant in the Plasticity lab at the MRC Cognition and Brain Sciences Unit at the University of Cambridge.

I recently completed my MPhil in Cognitive Neurosciences, during which I studied cortical tracking of speech in a selective attention paradigm as part of the Brain, Language and Bilingualism lab and under the supervision of Dr. Mirjana Bozic. Previously, I graduated with a B.Sc. in Computer Science from Lehigh University, USA.

Research

CV

My research is currently focused on bilingualism and selective attention.

Current work explores:

  • Cortical tracking of speech, specifically linguistic features (i.e. phonotactic probability, word surprisal, syntactic complexity), and how it is modulated by language experience and typological distance

Previous research experience includes:

  • Decoding temporal properties of visual stimuli from population-level dynamics captured by 2-photon calcium imaging of rodent primary visual cortex
  • Modelling longitudinal fMRI data with variational autoencoders to disentangle Alzheimer's-specific neurodegeneration from healthy ageing trajectories
  • Time-series based supervised anomaly detection in production systems, including the design and execution of experiments with artificially induced anomalies
